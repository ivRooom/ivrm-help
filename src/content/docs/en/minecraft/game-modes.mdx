The main game modes currently documented are listed below.

| Mode | Content | Minecraft | Platform | Java | Launcher |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main | Life, farming, cooking, building, technology, and MMO elements | 26.2 | Fabric 0.19.3 | 25 | CurseForge |
| ATM10 | Large technology, magic, and exploration Modpack | 1.21.1 | ATM10 7.2 / NeoForge | 21 | CurseForge |
| GTNH | Extremely difficult, long-term technology progression | 1.7.10 | GTNH 2.8.4 | Planned update to 25 | Prism Launcher |

:::caution[Subject to change]
Versions and launchers may change as operations are updated. Check the latest Discord announcement before joining.
:::

## Main

The main environment combines life, farming, cooking, building, technology, and MMO elements. It is the standard starting option for new IVRM Minecraft players.

## ATM10

A large Modpack environment containing many technology, magic, and exploration systems. It may require stronger PC hardware and longer startup time than Main.

## GTNH

A high-difficulty technology Modpack designed for long-term progression. Prism Launcher is the primary launcher, and installation differs from Main.

## Hardcore

A special-rule environment where the current world is saved and replaced with a new world when any participant dies.
