Follow these basic rules so that everyone can play safely and comfortably.

## Basic rules

1. Do not destroy or take another player’s buildings, equipment, or items without permission.
2. Do not harass other participants or make discriminatory remarks.
3. Do not exploit bugs, unauthorized tools, duplication, or similar methods.
4. Do not run devices that place excessive load on the server continuously without approval.
5. Follow the usage rules for shared facilities.
6. Do not share personal information or private connection information outside the community.
7. Report discovered problems to the administrators instead of hiding them.

:::danger[Prohibited activities]
Griefing, theft, unauthorized clients, intentional server disruption, and impersonation of administrators or participants are not allowed.
:::

## Building and land use

- Ask before building close to another player’s base.
- Check the impact on nearby areas before large construction or terrain changes.
- Do not remove abandoned-looking structures without administrator confirmation.

## Automation equipment

- Take special care with systems involving large numbers of mobs, items, or chunk loaders.
- Design unused equipment so it can be stopped.
- Follow administrator requests to stop or modify equipment.

:::caution[Official rules]
This page is an initial summary. When official rules are posted in Discord, the latest Discord version takes priority.
:::
