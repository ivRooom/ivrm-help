## How this search works

The assistant expands your question with related help terms and searches the published Help Center with Pagefind. Results are limited to IVRM documentation, and your question is not sent to an external AI service.

When the suggested guides do not solve the issue, use the **contact form** or **Discord** links shown in the assistant.
