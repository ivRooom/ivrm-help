---

The Discord channel structure may change. Follow the current channel names and descriptions in Discord.

## Common channel purposes

- **Announcements**: Important information from the administrators
- **Rules and information**: Participation requirements and community rules
- **General chat**: Community conversation
- **Minecraft**: Applications, updates, and questions
- **Support**: Help with bugs and other problems

When asking a question, use the channel that most closely matches the topic.
