---
title: Language and Theme Settings
description: Switch between Japanese and English and choose a light or dark theme
---

The IVRM Help Center supports Japanese and English content as well as light and dark themes.

## Change the language

Use the language menu in the page header to select **日本語** or **English**. You will be taken to the corresponding translated page.

## Change the theme

Use the theme menu in the page header to select one of the following options:

- **Auto**: Follow your device or browser appearance setting
- **Light**: Use a light background
- **Dark**: Use a dark background

:::note
When a translation is not yet available, content in the default language may be displayed.
:::
