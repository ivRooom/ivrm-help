The IVRM Help Center is a public manual for joining services, reviewing rules, following procedures, and resolving problems in the ivRooom community.

## What you can find here

- How to join the Minecraft servers
- Community rules and prohibited activities
- Discord channel and role guidance
- Common errors and solutions
- Information required when reporting a problem

:::note[Public information]
Operator-only information such as internal server architecture, credentials, and backup destinations is not published here.
:::
