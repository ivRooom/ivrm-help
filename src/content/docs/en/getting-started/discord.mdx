---
title: Join Discord
description: The first steps for joining the IVRM community
---

You need to join the IVRM Discord server to use community services such as the Minecraft servers.

## Steps

1. Open the Discord invitation from the [official IVRM website](https://ivrm.jp/).
2. Review the community rules in Discord.
3. Follow the instructions to complete verification or registration.
4. To join Minecraft, check the Minecraft-related channels.

:::caution
Invitation links and participation procedures may change. Always follow the latest information on the official website or in Discord.
:::
